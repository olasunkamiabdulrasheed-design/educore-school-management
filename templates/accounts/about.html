Our Story

Building the Future of Education Management

EduCore was born from a simple observation: schools needed a better way to manage their operations. Founded in 2013, we set out to create a platform that would simplify administration, empower teachers, and improve student outcomes.

Today, EduCore serves hundreds of schools across 20+ countries, helping them streamline everything from attendance tracking to fee management, all while maintaining the highest standards of security and reliability.
